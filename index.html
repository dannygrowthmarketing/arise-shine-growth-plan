26 · Quick distinctions & your questions

Ten pairs worth knowing cold

TAM / SAM / SOMAll beauty spend in Bengaluru → clients within ~3 km of each branch → the share you can win and measure in a year.
Beachhead vs wedgeBeachhead = the premium client in HRBR / Kalyan Nagar. Wedge = the free noindex + profile fix that opens the door.
Barriers vs switching costsBarriers = Adorn & Admire's 3,580 reviews. Switching costs = "my stylist" — the strongest lock-in you have.
Scarcity vs FOMOScarcity = a stylist's real Saturday slots. FOMO = the client's worry about missing the bridal window.
Anchoring vs framingAnchoring = ₹6,000 list next to ₹3,999. Framing = "fix my frizz" instead of "Women-Services".
Bandwagon vs mere exposureBandwagon = "most-booked treatment". Mere exposure = the same stylist face on Google, reels and ads.
Red vs Blue OceanRed = discount wars among 17 salons. Blue = late + valet + premium brands + named stylists.
Marketplace vs aggregatorSalonist / Fresha take bookings (marketplace). WeddingWire / magicpin list & compare (aggregator).
Conversion vs enquiry rateEnquiry = visitors who chat. Conversion = chats that become confirmed bookings.
CAC vs LTVCAC = cost to win a client. LTV = what that client is worth over the year — retention moves it most.

The rule: real scarcity, genuine reviews, accurate prices — honesty is what makes clients rebook.
